Belt length formula

For an open belt (both pulleys turning the same way), the most common approximate formula is:

L = 2C + 1.57 × (D + d) + (D − d)² / (4C)

Where L is the belt length, C the distance between shaft centers, D the large pulley diameter and d the small one. It holds when the center distance is much larger than the difference between the diameters.

Worked example

Pulleys of 200 mm and 100 mm with 500 mm between centers:

L = 2×500 + 1.57×(200+100) + (200−100)² / (4×500)
L = 1000 + 471 + 5 = 1476 mm

Pick the standard belt with the closest length and adjust the center distance to tension it.

Minimum and maximum center distance

As a rule of thumb, keep the center distance between 0.7 × (D + d) and 2 × (D + d). Too short reduces the wrap angle and the belt slips; too long causes vibration. Always leave adjustment travel to install and tension the belt.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate belt length?

Use L = 2C + 1.57(D + d) + (D − d)² / 4C, where C is the center distance and D and d are the pulley diameters.

Does the formula work for V-belts and timing belts?

It is a good approximation for both. Round the result to the manufacturer's standard length, and check the pitch for timing belts.

How do I find the center distance from a known belt?

Solve the length formula for C. The app does this when you leave the distance field blank.
